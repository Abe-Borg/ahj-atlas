# AHJ Atlas 1.11.0 — Canadian research and clearer project locations

This release includes all changes since 1.10.0, improving Canadian research, project location handling, and evidence reading.

## What's new

- **Choose the project's country beside its address.** New projects require **United States** or **Canada**. The form suggests a country from postal codes, states, provinces, territories, and trailing country names. If a definite address reading contradicts your choice, the app asks you to correct it before saving. A bare trailing **CA** remains ambiguous. Existing projects keep their saved country.
- **Correct the country with the address.** **Continue research** and **Review or extend report** now include **Country**. Changing it reopens jurisdiction research and dependent stages, keeps saved sources and recorded costs, and warns research that earlier findings may describe the previous country.
- **Research Canadian frameworks explicitly.** Canadian research, final review, and chat now check provincial and territorial adoption, municipal and special-land authorities, Canadian electrical and referenced standards, and French-language sources in Quebec. Fire protection research also checks applicable CAN/ULC and CSA references. Authorities and editions still require retrieved evidence.
- **Place searches closer to Canadian sites.** Research and chat can send the address's city, province or territory, and a representative time zone to web search. US address reading also handles all states and listed territories, dotted abbreviations, and full names more reliably, while avoiding street directions and street-type look-alikes.
- **Verify Canadian NFPA references.** Fire protection research checks NFPA editions against the applicable Canadian code's referenced-documents table rather than assuming the newest edition applies.
- **Describe sites in local terms.** Project forms use **Parcel number or site description**, accept US APNs and Canadian PIDs, roll or lot numbers and legal land descriptions, and show ZIP or postal code guidance. Canadian prompts use Canadian parcel examples.
- **Read contact evidence more reliably.** Email passage matching is bounded to avoid excessive work on long text. Windows builds now run the browser regression checks as well as deterministic tests and package audits.

## Limitations

Address reading is a suggestion and consistency check, not geocoding or proof of jurisdiction. A state or province without a postal or ZIP code only suggests a country. New projects support only the United States and Canada; older projects with another saved country retain it until changed.

Canadian web search uses one representative time zone per province or territory and does not send an unconfirmed Canadian country code. Provinces spanning multiple time zones may use a different local zone. Canadian framework notes identify what to investigate; they do not establish adoption or applicability. Findings, citations, and exported reports still require professional review and do not establish code compliance, legal applicability, or completeness. Automated regression checks use synthetic providers and do not verify live provider results. The app does not enforce spending limits; your Anthropic invoice is authoritative.

## Updating and Windows installation

From 1.7.0 or later, use **API & spending → App updates → Check for updates**, then **Download update** and **Restart and install** after this release is published. Earlier versions require a manual installation. Back up your workspace before upgrading and use the same Windows account to keep saved projects and the remembered credential.

For a manual installation, download `AHJ-Atlas-1.11.0-Windows-x64-Setup.exe` and `SHA256SUMS.txt` from this release, verify the SHA-256 checksum, close AHJ Atlas, and run the installer. The installer includes the app runtime. The optional JavaScript page renderer uses an installed Edge or Chrome browser.

The installer and executable are unsigned. Windows may show **Unknown publisher** or a SmartScreen warning. Confirm the download comes from this project's GitHub Release and verify its checksum before choosing whether to run it. Do not disable Windows security protections. A matching checksum confirms the published bytes; it does not prove the publisher's identity.

See the [Windows installation guide](https://github.com/Abe-Borg/ahj-atlas/blob/main/docs/windows-installation.md) for installation, in-app updates, backup, uninstall, and checksum steps.
